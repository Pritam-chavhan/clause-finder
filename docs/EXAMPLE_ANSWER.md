# Example output (real, unedited except trimmed at the end)

**Question (asked in everyday words, no clause numbers given):** "Can I extend my kitchen or bedroom into the open space beside my house?"

**Plain-language answer (AI-written, then checked by code)**

**No, you cannot extend your kitchen or bedroom into the open space beside your house. All exterior open spaces and required marginal open spaces must be kept permanently free from any erection and open to the sky.**

The regulations require that the open areas and side spaces around your house remain completely clear of any buildings or structures. This means you are not allowed to build extensions like a kitchen or bedroom into these open spaces. These areas must stay open to the sky at all times.

**The regulation says (word for word, checked against the clause text):**

> "Every exterior or interior, open space shall be kept free from any erection thereon and shall be kept open to sky" (clause 6.16.2)

> "The required marginal open spaces except permissible uses as per Regulation no. 6.7.3 shall be kept permanently open to sky." (clause 6.7.4)

The tool then prints the original clause text, page numbers and source below the answer.
